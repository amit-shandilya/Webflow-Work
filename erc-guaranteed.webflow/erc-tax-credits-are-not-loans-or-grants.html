ERC Tax Credits Are Not Loans or Grants, It’s a Tax Credit

The employee retention credit (ERC) is a tax credit and not a loan or a grant. Here’s the difference and what it means for you when you apply.

The employee retention credit, also known as ERC tax credit, is not a loan or a grant, it is a tax credit where you may get a refund on the qualified wages paid during the timeframe March 13, 2020 and September 30, 2021.  There are a few key differences between this tax credit, a grant, and a loan.

ERC Tax Credit Grants Loans
Requires the recipient to pay the money back No No Yes
Has interest on it No No Yes
Must use the funds for specific purposes No Yes Yes
Provided by the government and the private sector No Yes Yes

Tax Credits are refunds that are paid by the government and can be either a reduction in income or money back in your pocket to use as you see fit.  Grants and loans are also money given to you, but they must be used within the terms set by the issuer.  Tax credits and grants do not need to be paid back, but loans do and will likely require the borrower to pay interest on them.

Loans and grants can be for any amount of money, but the ERC tax credit is only available for the amount of wages collected during the time frame mentioned above, and on what you spent.  The more employees you paid while your business was impacted, the more you may be able to claim as long as you qualify for the program.

Grants are different from loans and ERC tax credits because there are a set amount of grants available where the amount of loans depends on the lender and the ERC tax credit can apply to anyone that qualifies.  Much like a grant, the ERC tax credit does require that you apply by a certain date as the program will close, this is different from a loan which will be available as long as a lender chooses to provide new ones.

ERC tax credits are similar to loans in that the amounts can and will change.  Loans could have fluctuating interest rates, and the amount you can claim on ERC tax credits will shrink the closer to the deadline to file your claim.  And just like a grant, you must get your application correct and submitted before this date occurs.

Grants, loans, and ERC tax credits are similar, but the ERC tax credit is not the same as a loan or a grant.  And now you know the differences and similarities.
